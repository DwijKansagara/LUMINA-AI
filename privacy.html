Privacy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Camera and microphone

LUMINA requests camera or microphone access only after you choose a mode. Frames, samples and model predictions are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by this application.

Snapshots

A snapshot is created only when you select “Save camera snapshot.” It is downloaded directly to your device.

Visit and appreciation counters

Each page load adds one aggregate view without saving a persistent visitor identifier. If you press the appreciation control, the browser creates a random value for this site and saves it in local storage so it can remember your progress and enforce the 20-press limit. The counter stores only salted one-way identifiers, the site name, reaction total, short-lived rate-limit records and timestamps. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track prevent the automatic view record. Read the shared details.

Network requests

The application downloads pinned machine-learning libraries from jsDelivr, model files from this site, and the engagement component from dwij-signal.vercel.app. AntiDeploy and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary request logs needed to deliver the site.
