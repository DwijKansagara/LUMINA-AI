Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Camera and microphone

LUMINA requests camera or microphone access only after you choose a mode. Frames, samples and model predictions are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by this application.

Snapshots

A snapshot is created only when you select “Save camera snapshot.” It is downloaded directly to your device.

Visit and appreciation counters

The engagement panel uses Dwij's portfolio counter. It converts the network address and browser user-agent already included in a normal request into a salted, one-way identifier and stores only that identifier, the site name, reaction total and timestamps. It sets no cookie or browser-storage identifier. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track prevent the automatic visit record. Read the shared details.

Network requests

The application downloads pinned machine-learning libraries from jsDelivr, model files from this site, and the engagement component from Dwij's portfolio domain. AntiDeploy and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary request logs needed to deliver the site.
