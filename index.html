Permissions and data

Nothing is uploaded by this page.

Camera frames, microphone samples, predictions, and snapshots are handled locally in the browser. The page downloads TensorFlow.js libraries from jsDelivr and model files from this repository. A snapshot is saved only when you request it.

Common questions

How LUMINA handles input.

Does LUMINA upload camera or microphone input?

No. Camera frames, microphone samples and model predictions are processed in this browser by the application.

When are permissions requested?

Only after you select camera or voice mode.

Can predictions be used for safety decisions?

No. LUMINA is an experimental learning prototype and its predictions can be incorrect.
