Cookies

Last updated: 28 September 2026

No application cookies

LUMINA does not set first-party cookies, use advertising trackers or run an analytics package. Its view counter stores no visitor identifier. If you press the appreciation control, a random first-party value is saved in local storage to remember that browser's progress and enforce the 20-press limit. No optional analytics, advertising or third-party tracking is loaded.

Hosting, libraries and counter

AntiDeploy delivers the site, jsDelivr delivers the pinned machine-learning libraries, and dwij-signal.vercel.app supplies the counter component and totals. Those providers may handle standard request information under their own policies.

Changes

This notice and the consent flow will be updated before any cookie-based feature or optional analytics is introduced.
