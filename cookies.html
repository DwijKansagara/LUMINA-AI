Cookies

Last updated: 28 September 2026

No application cookies

LUMINA does not set first-party cookies, use advertising trackers or run an analytics package. Its anonymous visit and appreciation counter does not write an identifier to browser storage. A cookie-consent banner is unnecessary while this remains true.

Hosting, libraries and counter

AntiDeploy delivers the site, jsDelivr delivers the pinned machine-learning libraries, and dwij-portfolio.antideploy.com supplies the counter component and totals. Those providers may handle standard request information under their own policies.

Changes

This notice and the consent flow will be updated before any cookie-based feature or optional analytics is introduced.
